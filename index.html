Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.bangeltech.easy.browser

Your privacy matters to us. Easy Browser is a browser application developed by BangelTech that helps you browse the internet while giving you control over your browsing experience.

1. Browsing History

Websites you visit may be stored locally on your device to provide browsing history and convenience. You can clear your browsing history using the browser's available settings.

2. Cookies and Website Data

Websites may store cookies and other data to remember preferences, maintain sessions, and provide website functionality. You may manage or clear this data through browser settings.

3. Personal Information

Do not enter sensitive personal information on untrusted websites. Information submitted to a website is handled according to that website's privacy policy.

4. Permissions

Easy Browser may request permissions such as camera, microphone, location, or file access when required by a feature or website. Permissions should only be granted when you are comfortable with their use.

5. Downloads

Files downloaded through the browser may be saved to your device. Check downloaded files before opening them, especially when their source is unknown.

6. Third-Party Websites

Easy Browser can open third-party websites. BangelTech does not control the content, security, or privacy practices of those websites. Please review their policies before sharing personal information.

7. Data Security

We recommend keeping your device and browser updated. The actual security and handling of data depend on the features implemented in this app and the services it uses.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. Any revised policy should be published with an updated effective date where appropriate.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact the app developer, BangelTech, using the contact information provided on the app's store listing.
